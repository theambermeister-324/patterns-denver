🏛️ LLM Council Verdict

Rebuilding Patterns Denver in IBM Carbon — POC

Should we proceed with a proof-of-concept that rebuilds the workshop site's home page in IBM's Carbon Design System — a standalone Vite + React + TypeScript @carbon/react app in a subfolder, redesigned (not 1:1 ported), deployed to a new Netlify URL — to test the thesis "Can AI rebuild our site in Carbon?"
Counciled 2026-06-12 · 5 advisors · anonymized peer review · chairman synthesis

Council Verdict

Where the council agrees

  • Home-page-only + standalone app + new URL is the right shape. The plumbing is sound.
  • The real thesis is "can AI produce idiomatic Carbon" — not "can AI scaffold a Carbon app." Hardcoded hex where a token belongs = thesis failed, and DS practitioners spot it instantly.
  • The proof artifact is the build process, not the deployed URL.

Where the council clashes

Redesign vs. 1:1 port. Contrarian + Outsider argue a free redesign is unfalsifiable (looks good → "AI freelanced"; looks bad → "Carbon doesn't fit") and want a portable side-by-side. The counter-case: a faithful port of friendly purple content into enterprise Carbon looks worse, and "AI speaks Carbon natively" is the more interesting claim. This is the one decision that reshapes the plan.

Blind spots the peer round caught

  • Conflict of interest — Knapsack sells DS governance, yet the POC has no Knapsack product in the loop ("where's your platform?").
  • Carbon is IBM's governed/trademarked system — worth a glance before public use.
  • The build process (live or recorded) is more convincing than a finished screenshot.

The recommendation

Proceed — with three amendments: (1) define an idiomatic-Carbon pass/fail rubric before building and grade against it; (2) capture wall-clock, interventions, and where Carbon lacked a component; (3) adopt the Executor's build specifics (sass-embedded + loadPaths + quietDeps/silenceDeprecations; @ibm/plex; verify a styled Button first).

The one thing to do first: Write the Carbon-fidelity rubric before any code. It's the difference between a demo and a vibe.

Advisor responses

Contrarian The test is rigged to lie to you

Testing the wrong thesis: the POC measures "can AI scaffold a Carbon app," not governance (respecting tokens, reusing components, not drifting). A greenfield page can't fail because there's nothing to drift from. "Redesign not port" makes the result unfalsifiable. A room of practitioners will spot wrong $spacing-05, misused Tiles, wrong type tokens — AI is good at "looks Carbon-ish," bad at "is Carbon-correct," and faking it damages what Knapsack sells. Stack is gratuitous risk. Fix: 1:1 port, define pass/fail before building, expert grader.

First Principles You're asking the wrong question

You already know AI can do it mechanically. The real question: "Can Knapsack credibly say AI + a DS produces idiomatic, on-brand output faster than humans?" A clean subfolder is the easy case; customers have messy systems. Measure fidelity (real components/tokens vs hand-rolled), speed with honesty (log wall-clock + interventions), and constraint adherence (what does AI do when Carbon lacks a component?). Pick the most awkward section, not the cleanest. Test where it breaks.

Expansionist It's a productized motion, not a rebuild

The valuable output is the transcript — proof AI can stand up a foreign, opinionated enterprise DS idiomatically in an afternoon. That's a sales asset and a wedge into every company with a real design system. Don't stop at one page: the same harness rebuilds in Material, Fluent, Polaris — a live "design-system switcher." The real product surface is AI-assisted DS migration. Capture metrics; build so the second design system is trivial to add.

Outsider One corporate page ≠ "rebuilt our site"

"Rebuild OUR site" vs "redesign it" are different tests. A workshop event site is friendly, human; Carbon is the suit-and-tie enterprise look — why dress a fun event in IBM's uniform? One page of seven, redesigned so you can't compare it, on a URL nobody visits = a single screenshot. Honest headline: "AI built one new page in a corporate style." Better: rebuild the home page to look like the current one, side by side. Don't let scaffolding become the story.

Executor Three friction points, here's the fast path

Carbon + Vite Sass is the #1 time-sink: @use '@carbon/react' fails out of the box — use sass-embedded, set loadPaths: ['node_modules'], quietDeps + silenceDeprecations. IBM Plex: @ibm/plex, not Google Fonts. Netlify subfolder is trivial (base/command/publish). Hero: Carbon has no hero component — build from grid + type tokens + Button. Fastest path: scaffold, fix Sass FIRST, verify one styled Button, then build the page.

Decisions taken (post-verdict)

Fidelity test Redesign + rubric

Keep the Carbon-native redesign, but add an explicit idiomatic-Carbon pass/fail rubric and grade the output against it.

Knapsack angle Design for the switcher

Separate content (content.ts) from the Carbon presentation layer so a second design system swaps in trivially — leaning into the on-brand AI-assisted DS-migration story.
