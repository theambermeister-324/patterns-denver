## This week: three things to try

**1. Point the sandbox at your real docs**
Clone the workshop repo, drop in a component spec or README from your actual system, and run `/ingest all` → `/query What are the documentation gaps?`

The sandbox doesn't care if your docs are incomplete. That's the point.

**2. Run the new-engineer test**
`/query What would a new engineer need to know that isn't in these docs?`

Whatever Claude can't answer from your docs — that's where someone is asking a human right now. That gap is your first automation target.

**3. Share one finding with your team**
Not a demo. Not a proposal. One sentence: "I ran AI against our Button docs and it couldn't tell me when to use the ghost variant." That's the conversation starter.
