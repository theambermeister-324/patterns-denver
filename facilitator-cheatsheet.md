## The 3 Commands (what participants run, in order)

| Step | Command | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/setup` | "Setup complete. Ready to ingest." |
| 2 | `/ingest all` | Files listed, then "Ingestion complete. X pages created." |
| 3 | `/query [their question]` | A response with bullet points and a summary |

If you see all three — they're in good shape. Walk away.

## If Something Goes Wrong

**They see a red error after typing a command:**
> *"Let me take a look at that — hang on one second."*
> Text Amber. Don't try to fix it yourself.

**`/setup` runs but nothing seems to happen:**
> *"That might just be a slow first run. Try `/ingest all` next and see if it picks up."*

**`/query` returns nothing or says "no pages found":**
> *"Did `/ingest all` finish? Sometimes it needs a minute. Try running it again: `/ingest all`"*

**Claude Code isn't opening at all:**
> *"Let's try this — close the terminal, reopen it, go back to the `patterns-denver` folder, and type `claude` again."*
> If still nothing: text Amber immediately. This is the one that needs real help.

**Participant says "I don't know what to ask":**
> *"That's actually the most interesting place to be. Open `prompt-templates.md` — there are 6 starter questions on page 1. Pick the one that makes you most nervous."*
